Why is data quality important for analysis?;Poor data quality leads to invalid conclusions and results.
How can you calculate the percentage of missing values in a dataset using pandas?;Use `isna().mean() * 100` on a DataFrame or Series.
What does it mean if missing data is not random?;It suggests a pattern or systematic reason for the absence of values.

What are the three main approaches to handling missing values?;Remove rows, fill with imputed values, or keep as missing and report.
How should you interpret missing ratings in a dataset?;They indicate listings without any reviews, not random errors.
What is bias in data, and how does it differ from random error?;Bias is systematic skewing of results in one direction, unlike random errors which are unpredictable.
How can you detect duplicates in a dataset using pandas?;Use `duplicated()` method on a column like ID.
What should you check when validating values in a dataset?;Ensure values make sense given the column names and real-world expectations.
Why might "zero bathrooms" be misleading in a dataset?;It could indicate missing information or different data collection practices rather than actual zero bathrooms.
How do you identify unreasonable values in a dataset?;Look for extreme or logically inconsistent entries based on context.
What are the three core questions to ask about any dataset?;What is missing? Who or what is not in the table? Who made the data and why?
