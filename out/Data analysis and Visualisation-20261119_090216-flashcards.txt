What are the three key aspects of data quality?;Completeness, correctness, and consistency are the three key aspects of data quality.

Why is data quality important in analysis?;Poor data quality leads to misleading conclusions, making valid interpretations and decisions based on the data difficult.

How can missing data affect analysis results?;Missing data can introduce bias and reduce the reliability of analysis outcomes if not handled properly.

What is the difference between bias and random errors in data?;Bias is systematic and consistently pushes results in one direction, while random errors can cancel out and do not follow a pattern.

What does it mean when data has bias?;Bias refers to systematic deviations from the truth that consistently skew results in a particular direction.

How might missing prices be related to listing availability?;Missing prices are often found in listings with zero available days, suggesting those listings are not actively listed on the website.

What is an example of a misleading value in data?;A "zero bathroom" listing might actually have private bathrooms or the value may not have been recorded properly.

Why should you document data quality issues in your analysis?;Documentation ensures transparency and helps readers understand the scope, limitations, and validity of your conclusions.

What are the three main strategies for handling missing data?;Remove rows, fill with imputed values, or keep as missing while documenting the issue.

What is a key principle when choosing how to handle missing data?;It's more important to make a deliberate decision based on understanding rather than which approach you choose.

How can duplicate records impact analysis?;Duplicates can skew results by inflating counts and leading to incorrect interpretations of the dataset.

What are the four essential questions for checking data quality before analysis?;1. How much is missing and where? 2. Are there duplicates? 3. Are there impossible values? 4. Do values mean something different than their column names suggest?

Why should you be cautious about interpreting values that appear correct but may have hidden meanings?;Values can be technically accurate yet misleading if the column name or context doesn't fully explain what they represent.

How does understanding data quality help in making decisions?;It prevents overconfidence in results and ensures appropriate comparisons, leading to more thoughtful analysis and clearer communication of findings.

What is a common issue with rating data when no reviews exist?;All listings without reviews also have missing ratings, meaning the rating column only includes reviewed listings.

How can you improve transparency in reporting price data with missing values?;Instead of just stating a median price, specify how many listings were included and what percentage of total that represents.

What is the ultimate goal of addressing data quality issues?;To systematically identify problems, understand their implications, make informed decisions about analysis, and communicate limitations clearly to stakeholders.
