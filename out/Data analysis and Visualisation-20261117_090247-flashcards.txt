How do you ensure that date columns are properly converted to datetime objects when reading a CSV file in pandas?;Use the `parse_dates` parameter in `pd.read_csv()` and specify the column names that contain dates.

What is the difference between mean and median as measures of central tendency?;The mean is affected by extreme values and represents the arithmetic average, while the median is the middle value in an ordered dataset and is resistant to outliers.

Why might the median be preferred over the mean when analyzing data with outliers?;Because the median is not influenced by extreme values and provides a better representation of typical values in skewed distributions.

What does the interquartile range (IQR) measure in a dataset?;The IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data, calculated as the difference between the third and first quartiles.

How are outliers identified using the IQR rule?;Outliers are values that fall below Q1 - 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR, where Q1 and Q3 are the first and third quartiles.

What is the recommended approach for handling outliers in a dataset?;Mark outliers without removing them to maintain data integrity and allow flexibility in analysis.

Why is it important to compare statistical measures with and without outliers?;Because outliers can significantly skew mean and standard deviation, while median remains stable, helping to assess data distribution accurately.

How do you calculate percentiles and quartiles in pandas?;Use the `quantile()` method on a Series or DataFrame, specifying the desired percentile values (e.g., 0.25 for Q1).

What does a correlation coefficient close to zero indicate about the relationship between two variables?;It indicates that there is no meaningful linear relationship between the variables.

How can confounding variables affect correlation analysis?;They can create spurious relationships or mask true associations, leading to incorrect conclusions about causation.

What are three key practices for maintaining data integrity during analysis?;Verify data types after reading, check for missing values, and mark outliers rather than removing them.
