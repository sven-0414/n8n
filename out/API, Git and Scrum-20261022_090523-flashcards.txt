What is the main principle behind agile software development?;Agile emphasizes iterative development, small steps, and rapid feedback through short cycles called sprints.

How does agile differ from the waterfall method?;Agile uses iterative cycles with early feedback, while waterfall follows a sequential approach where each phase must be completed before moving to the next.

What are the four core values of the Agile Manifesto?;The four core values are individuals and interactions over processes and tools, working software over comprehensive documentation, customer collaboration over contract negotiation, and responding to change over following a plan.

What is Scrum in the context of agile development?;Scrum is the most popular agile framework that provides specific roles, events, and artifacts to implement agile principles effectively.

Who are the three main roles in a Scrum team?;The three main roles are the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers (which includes programmers, testers, designers, etc.).

What are the five key events in a Scrum sprint cycle?;The five events are Sprint, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective.

What is a user story in agile development?;A user story is a simple description of a feature written from the user's perspective using the format: "As a [user], I want [feature] so that [reason]."

What is the purpose of the Definition of Done (DoD) in Scrum?;The Definition of Done is a checklist that defines when work is truly complete, ensuring consistency and quality across all delivered increments.

How are story points used in agile estimation?;Story points are a relative measure of effort using the Fibonacci sequence to represent increasing uncertainty and help teams estimate work complexity.

What is the main goal of backlog refinement?;Backlog refinement prepares items for future sprints by reviewing, clarifying, and breaking down product backlog items into manageable tasks.

What are the key differences between Scrum and Kanban?;Scrum uses fixed sprints with defined roles and goals, while Kanban focuses on continuous flow with work-in-progress limits and no fixed iterations.

What is a common mistake in implementing Scrum?;A common mistake is treating the daily scrum as a status report to management instead of a team coordination meeting.

Why is understanding agile important for developers?;Understanding agile is crucial for job interviews, real-world teamwork, and career advancement since most development teams use these methodologies.

How can solo developers apply agile concepts?;Solo developers can apply agile by doing daily progress updates, breaking large tasks into small steps, seeking early feedback, and reflecting on their processes for improvement.
