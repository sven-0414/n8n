What is the difference between mean and median?;The mean is the average of all values, calculated by summing all values and dividing by the number of values, while the median is the middle value when data is arranged in order from smallest to largest.

How does the presence of outliers affect mean and median?;The mean is affected by extreme values (outliers), whereas the median is robust against outliers because it only depends on the position of values, not their actual values.

Why is the median often preferred over the mean in skewed distributions?;The median better represents the "typical" value in skewed data because it is not influenced by extreme values, unlike the mean which can be pulled in the direction of the skew.

What are percentiles and why are they useful?;Percentiles divide data into 100 equal parts, helping to understand data distribution and identify outliers by showing how values are spread across the dataset.

What is the interquartile range (IQR) and how is it calculated?;The interquartile range measures the spread of the middle 50% of data points, calculated as Q3 minus Q1 (the third quartile minus the first quartile).

How do you identify outliers using the IQR rule?;Outliers are identified by calculating the lower fence (Q1 - 1.5 × IQR) and upper fence (Q3 + 1.5 × IQR), with values outside this range considered outliers.

What are the three main approaches to handling outliers in data analysis?;The three main approaches are removing them, modifying them, or keeping and marking them, with marking being the most honest approach for analysis.

Why is it important to consider group differences when analyzing data?;Group analysis helps identify variations between categories (like room types) and provides deeper insights into how different segments behave differently in the dataset.

What does correlation measure and what do its values indicate?;Correlation measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship between two variables, ranging from -1 (perfect negative correlation) to +1 (perfect positive correlation), with 0 indicating no correlation.

Why can't correlation be used to determine causation?;Correlation only shows that two variables are related or move together, but does not prove that one variable causes the other; there may be confounding factors or reverse causality.
