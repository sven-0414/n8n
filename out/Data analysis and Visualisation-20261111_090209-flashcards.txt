What is the primary purpose of data cleaning in data analysis?;Data cleaning ensures accurate analysis and reliable results by identifying and correcting errors, inconsistencies, and missing values in datasets.

What are the key steps involved in the data cleaning process?;The process involves examining the data to identify problems, fixing them systematically, and documenting all steps taken during the cleaning process.

Why is it important to clean data before performing analysis?;Clean data ensures accurate analysis and reliable results, as decisions made during cleaning determine what the final numbers mean.

What are some common issues found in real-world datasets?;Common issues include text format dates, text format prices with currency symbols and commas, empty or inconsistent columns, and inconsistent text formatting.

How do you standardize category values in a dataset using pandas?;You use pandas string methods like `str.strip()`, `str.lower()`, and `str.replace()` to remove whitespace, normalize case, and replace variations with consistent formats.

What is the difference between `str.replace()` and `.replace()` in pandas?;`str.replace()` replaces parts of text within values (like removing currency symbols), while `.replace()` replaces entire values (like changing one category name to another).

How do you clean text format prices that contain currency symbols, spaces, and commas?;You strip whitespace, remove currency symbols, replace internal spaces, and convert commas to decimal points before converting the column to numeric type.

What happens when you use `pd.to_datetime()` with invalid date formats and `errors='coerce'`?;Invalid dates are converted to NaN (Not a Time) instead of raising errors, allowing processing to continue without interruption.

How can you identify and remove duplicate records in a dataset?;Use the `duplicated()` method to identify duplicates and `drop_duplicates()` to remove them, optionally specifying which columns to check for duplication.

What is the best practice for handling missing values during data cleaning?;Always document the reason for missing data, handle it appropriately (remove, impute, or flag), and validate that the approach makes sense for the analysis.

How do you verify that your data cleaning steps were successful?;You can cross-check totals, verify consistency of values, and ensure calculated columns make logical sense after cleaning.

What should you do before checking for duplicates in a dataset?;Clean text data first by removing extra spaces and normalizing capitalization to ensure accurate duplicate detection.

How do you remove an entirely empty column from a dataset?;Use the `drop(columns=['column_name'])` method to remove columns that contain no useful data.

What is the significance of documenting decisions made during data cleaning?;Documentation ensures transparency, allows for reproducibility, and helps others understand how the final numbers were derived.

How do you calculate total revenue for each order line after cleaning?;Multiply `unit_price` by `quantity` to create a new column called `total`.

What are some validation techniques used after cleaning data?;Cross-check totals, verify consistency of values, and ensure calculated results make logical sense in the context of the dataset.

Why should you be cautious when using `pd.to_numeric()` with text data?;Because it can convert invalid entries to NaN instead of raising errors, which might mask underlying issues in the data.

What are some common pitfalls to avoid during data cleaning?;Not handling missing values properly, converting data types without error handling, and checking for duplicates before cleaning text fields.

How does data cleaning impact future analysis workflows?;Cleaned data provides a reliable foundation for deeper analysis, enabling accurate calculations of revenue by category, seasonal trends, and customer purchasing patterns.
