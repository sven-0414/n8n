What is the primary purpose of data cleaning in data analysis?;Data cleaning ensures that datasets are free from errors and inconsistencies, making the results of analysis reliable and trustworthy.

Why is it important to never modify the original dataset during cleaning?;Modifying the original dataset can lead to loss of information and prevents others from reproducing your work or checking your process.

How should you handle missing values when cleaning data?;Missing values should be analyzed to understand their cause, and then handled appropriately—either by imputation, removal, or keeping them as they may carry meaningful information.

What are the main types of problems found in real-world datasets?;Real-world datasets often contain text columns stored as numbers, inconsistent capitalization or spacing, missing data points, and duplicate entries.

How do you standardize text data such as categories in a dataset?;Text data can be standardized by stripping whitespace, converting to lowercase, correcting spelling variations, and applying title case where appropriate.

What steps are involved in cleaning numeric data like unit prices stored as text?;Numeric data should have currency symbols removed, internal spaces stripped, decimal separators converted correctly, and then converted to a proper numeric type using pd.to_numeric().

How do you convert text-based dates into usable datetime objects in pandas?;Use pd.to_datetime() function to parse text dates into datetime objects, handling conversion errors gracefully with the errors='coerce' parameter.

What is the recommended approach for removing duplicate rows from a dataset?;Identify duplicates using duplicated(), then remove them with drop_duplicates(keep='first') while resetting the index afterward.

Why should empty columns be removed during data cleaning?;Empty columns provide no value to analysis and can clutter datasets, so they should be removed to improve clarity and reduce storage needs.

How do you create calculated columns after cleaning your dataset?;After cleaning, you can create new columns by performing operations on existing ones, such as multiplying unit price by quantity to get total order value.

What are the benefits of grouping and aggregating cleaned data?;Grouping and aggregating allow for meaningful insights like revenue by category or monthly sales trends, which help inform business decisions.
